Instructions for using the current and voltage sensor:
----------------------------------------------------------------


Specifications:
---------------
Current: -8A min / +8A max
Voltage:  0V min / +26V max

----------------------------------------------------------------

IMPORTANT:
NEVER CONNECT A NEGATIVE VOLTAGE BETWEEN VIN+ AND GND
NEVER CONNECT THE TEENSY POWER SUPPLY IN REVERSE

----------------------------------------------------------------



LED Indicators:
---------------
Solid green: data recording in progress
Solid red: writing data to SD card
Alternating red-green blinking: waiting to start (see red paddle switch command)
Green blinking: data recording is complete, data writing is about to begin
Red blinking: data writing is complete, will restart either recording or standby
No light 2s after power-on: SD card not detected

-> Never disconnect the power when the LED is solid red; all other moments are safe.


Switches:
---------
Paddle switch toward USB = recording in progress
              toward outside = on standby

dipswitch #   1 - HIGH  (OFF)       0 - LOW (ON)
dipswitch 1   current or voltage  - current and voltage      choice of number of measured signals
dipswitch 2   current             - voltage                  signal selection, ignored if "current and voltage"
dipswitch 3   fast sampling       - slow sampling            500Hz - 5Hz (current and voltage) or 1000Hz - 10Hz (current or voltage)
dipswitch 4   short recording     - long recording           short=2000 measurements, long=20000 measurements
dipswitch 5   active filtering    - no filter                zero phase low-pass 0.1Fs 4th order Butterworth
dipswitch 6   NOT USED


Saving:
-------
Files written to the SD card are in CSV format and named "Measures***date***_***time***.csv"


Notes:
------
- LOAD: IN+ MUST BE POSITIVE WITH RESPECT TO GND, OTHERWISE INA219 WILL BE DESTROYED
- TEENSY: VIN MUST BE POSITIVE WITH RESPECT TO GND, OTHERWISE TEENSY WILL BE DESTROYED
- Recording durations:
    2 signals = 4s (fast-short) or 40s (fast-long)
    2 signals = 6min40s (slow-short) or 1hr6min40s (TO BE TESTED slow-long)
    1 signal = 2s (fast-short) or 20s (fast-long)
    1 signal = 3min20s (slow-short) or 33min20s (TO BE TESTED slow-long)
- The INA219 has a bandwidth of 1000 Hz at -3dB, so around 100-200Hz max for realistic accuracy.
- The date and time are saved by a CR2032 battery powering the Teensy's RTC. Should last around 5 years.
